{# SPONSOR 2026-09-02 §I4 — "a prompt allow the hiring manager to list out special skills, experience they are looking for. These might or might not be in the job posting." Its OWN box, deliberately separate from Description. `description_raw` is the JD of record — the posting as published — and folding the manager's note into it would make the two indistinguishable forever afterwards, which is precisely what the shortlist has to be able to tell apart when it explains a rank. The hint states the two things a manager cannot infer from the box itself: that these count as required unless softened, and that this is where a requirement the posting missed actually belongs. #}

Special skills or experience you are looking for, whether or not the posting mentions them. Anything you state plainly is treated as required; write "nice to have", "bonus" or "ideally" to soften one. These are scored separately from the posting, so the shortlist can show which requirements came from you.
